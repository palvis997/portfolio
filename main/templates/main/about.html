Who I Am

I am Pavis Mugo Muruga, a second-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student at Kirinyaga University in Kenya (Expected Graduation: 2028).

My passion lies at the intersection of practical software development, dependable systems architecture, and IT support. I enjoy transforming real-world administrative and community challenges into functional, clean, and accessible web solutions using Python, Django, and modern web standards.

During my 13-week industrial attachment at the ICT Authority of Kenya, I had the privilege of working on mission-critical projects including the Library Management System, ICT Asset Management System, and GBV / Digital Safe Space System, alongside day-to-day user support and network maintenance.
